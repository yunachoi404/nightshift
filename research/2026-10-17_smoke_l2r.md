# Smoke test: greybox -> FLUX.2 Klein first frame -> LTX 2.5 Layout-To-Render (2026-10-17)

## Setup
- Layout: `scripts/previz/greybox_test.py`. Street of boxes, a cylinder+sphere mannequin walking away 4 m,
  camera dollying 6.5 m forward behind it at 28 mm. 960x544, 97 frames, 24 fps.
- First frame: `workflows/look_edit_klein4b.api.json` on clay frame 1, prompt "turn this grey 3D blockout into a
  photoreal film still, keep the exact camera and composition...". 24 s.
- Render: `workflows/layout_to_render.api.json` (Lightricks two-stage distilled graph, int8 transformer and
  text encoder), seed 42, through comfy-sdk -> comfy-api-proxy -> our ComfyUI on the DGX Spark (46 GB scheduler budget).
- Output: 1920x1088, 97 frames, 24 fps. **317 s** wall clock for the job, model load included.
- Files (gitignored): `outputs/smoke/render/look_layout_render.mp4` (first frame | layout | render).

## What happened
- The whole pipeline runs end to end through the Comfy SDK. Picture quality is high: wet asphalt, reflections,
  haze, a walking figure with a stable reflection, no flicker.
- The camera moves forward (frame-to-frame change rises and falls with the layout's eased dolly).
- **Composition followed the first frame, not the layout.** Klein re-framed the scene: woman centred and about
  twice the mannequin's size, buildings re-laid. The render kept Klein's framing: the woman stays centred and the
  same size all shot long, while in the layout she sits right of centre and grows about 1.5x as the camera gains.

## Takeaways for the app
1. The first frame must match the layout's composition exactly. That is the main sub-problem to solve, not the
   video model. Candidates: stronger structure-preserving edit (Klein 9B, Qwen Image Edit 2511), depth/edge
   control (Qwen Image 2.1 DiffSynth controlnet is in core), or FLUX 3 Klein's bounding-box layout prompt
   (`Flux3LayoutPrompt` + `CreateBoundingBoxes`): our stage knows every object's projected box, so it can pass them.
2. Keep subjects at a quarter of the frame or more; this mannequin was too small to anchor framing.
3. ~5 min per 4 s shot at full res. A stage-1-only preview (960x544) should be much faster; measure it.
4. SDK quirks: `Output.size_bytes` reports 0, and `job.outputs` includes the LoadVideo input. Filter to the
   SaveVideo node in the runner.
