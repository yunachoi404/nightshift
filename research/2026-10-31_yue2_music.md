# YuE2-3B against MiniMax Music 3, 2026-10-31

The owner finds MiniMax Music 3's songs weak. This lab asks whether YuE2-3B can replace it on the big NVIDIA and
unified-memory tiers. Every number is from the shared DGX Spark through the Comfy SDK, one job at a time. Raw
results for every song are in [2026-10-31_yue2_music.json](2026-10-31_yue2_music.json).

**Decision: switch.** With its authors' own instrumental recipe, YuE2 made 22 instrumentals. Whisper heard words in
one, a short phrase over strings that may just be the strings. MiniMax Music 3 had words in 4 of 10 songs on the same
prompts and seed, and its vocal stem was never silent. YuE2 scores higher on prompt match and enjoyment, about the
same on production quality, and makes a 30-second song in less than half the time. ACE-Step stays on the smaller
tiers.

## Setup

- ComfyUI v0.41.3 runs YuE2 with its built-in nodes: YuE2 Generate ABC, YuE2 Generate Music and Empty YuE2 Latent
  Audio. No ComfyUI upgrade.
- The model is one 3.96 GB file, `yue2_3b_int8_convrot.safetensors`, from
  [Comfy-Org/YuE2](https://huggingface.co/Comfy-Org/YuE2/tree/2f76ca75e6ee094169de899cc7fc99d6887e2196). It went into
  our own `comfyui/models/checkpoints/` after a SHA256 check. The lab did not touch the shared model folders.
- Settings follow the official "YuE2: Text to Music" template: 32 diffusion steps, dpm_2, sgm_uniform.
- Prompts are the app's own: the song popover's "Slow piano, warm, a little hopeful", "Lo-fi beat, soft rain, warm
  late-night city feel", the music video placeholder "Driving home at 2 am, the city asleep, missing someone", the
  What If template's instrumental cue, and the engine tests' "slow strings, storm". Seed 42 for both models, plus
  seeds 43 and 44 for the shipped recipe.
- Runner: `scripts/spark/music_lab.py` through `scripts/spark/bench.py`. bench.py releases the models before each
  job, so every time includes loading. Peak is the scheduler's process memory, sampled each second.
- Checks: `scripts/analyze_music_ab.py`. Loudness is EBU R128 through ffmpeg. For vocals, Demucs (htdemucs) splits a
  vocal stem and Whisper small.en transcribes it. CLAP (LAION, music checkpoint) scores how well a song matches its
  prompt. Audiobox Aesthetics (Meta) predicts content enjoyment (CE) and production quality (PQ) from 1 to 10. These
  are proxies. They do not replace listening. Both checks repeat exactly: Demucs runs on the CPU with no random
  shift, and CLAP averages every 10-second window instead of one random crop.

MiniMax Music 3 sang on the app's prompts. On the drive prompt it chanted "Yay!" for 15 seconds. On the piano prompt
it sang "It's been a while and you've been waiting". Its vocal stem was never 40 dB below the mix, in any of 11
songs. This matches the earlier finding that it sang in seven of eight 90-second songs.

## Why YuE2 sings, and the fix

YuE2 is a song model. With empty lyrics it still sang made-up words in 3 of 5 songs at 30 seconds and in one of two
60-second songs. With its ABC planner it sang about as often, 4 of 10 against 3 of 10. The planner writes a vocal
melody for every verse and chorus. Adding "Instrumental, no vocals." to the style made it worse, 5 of 5. An
`[Instrumental]` lyrics tag helped but did not fix it.

The YuE2 authors publish an instrumental recipe in the `yue2-music` skill in their repository. It has three steps.

1. Plan an ABC score from the style, with lyrics that are only section tags: `[Intro] [Verse] [Chorus] [Outro]`.
2. Move every note of the vocal part to the instrument part. The vocal part keeps only rests and chords.
3. Render that score with the style "Instrumental, <prompt>, no vocals, no singing, no choir, no spoken words." and
   the score's section tags as lyrics.

Our node `NightshiftYuE2Instrumental` does step 2 with the authors' MIT code, pinned and vendored in
`comfy_nodes/nightshift/yue2_score/`. Lyrics that hold words pass through unchanged, so a song with words still
works. A plan the converter refuses falls back to no plan and empty lyrics. All 15 lab plans converted without it.

With the moved melody, 22 songs ran: 5 prompts at 15 and 30 seconds, two more seeds at 30 seconds, and two at 60
seconds. In 17, the vocal stem is at least 40 dB below the mix, which is silence. The other five are three strings
songs and two What If songs. Demucs often files bowed strings and lead synths as vocals. In one of them, strings at
seed 43, Whisper heard "I love you, oh". That one needs a listen. It may be a voice, or just the strings.

## Time and memory

On the Spark, the plan and the render ran as two SDK jobs, because loading a new node means restarting our ComfyUI,
which the judges' preview also uses. Their sum is the time above. That is an upper bound, since the shipped graph
loads the model once. Planning takes 8 to 46 seconds. The drive prompt writes the longest scores. Rendering takes 11 seconds
for 15 seconds of music, 16 to 22 for 30 and 26 to 32 for 60. The recipe stores the mean for a 30-second song, 34.3
seconds, and the highest peak, 10.1 GB. MiniMax Music 3 took 80 seconds and 28 GB for the same length.

The shipped graph itself, node included, ran end to end through the SDK and comfy-api-proxy on an M4 Max Mac, with
ComfyUI v0.41.3 on MPS. It made a 15-second piano song with no words heard and a vocal stem at -62 dB. It took 431
seconds. That is
far too slow for the Apple tiers, which keep ACE-Step.

## Lyrics

YuE2 sings given lyrics well. The lyrics were "Streetlights hum a quiet tune / Shadows dancing with the moon / Take
the long way home tonight / Hold the city in our light". Without a plan, Whisper heard "Streetlights home, a quiet
tune. Shadows dancing with the moon. Take the low away home tonight. Hold the city in our light". A future "song
with words" option could use it. The shipped graph already sings lyrics that hold words. The app does not offer that
yet.

## Limits

- YuE2 renders the opening of a full song plan. The plans ran 50 to 313 seconds, so a 30-second song is the intro
  and the start of a verse.
- A song can be shorter than asked when the plan is shorter. One of two 60-second requests came back at 50 seconds.
  A music video then cuts to the song. Every 15- and 30-second request came back at full length.
- Loudness varies more than MiniMax's, from -28 to -14 LUFS. The editor sets the song's level and fades it, so
  exports are even.
- Audiobox's production complexity score is lower with the moved melody. It is 4.6 at 30 seconds, against 5.8 for
  MiniMax Music 3 and 5.6 for YuE2 without a plan. The arrangement is simpler.
- MiniMax Music 3 ran one seed per prompt. The proxies cannot rank melody or groove. I trust the word counts more
  than the scores. Listen before calling YuE2 the better composer.

  commercial use with conditions; this switch gives that up on the two tiers.
