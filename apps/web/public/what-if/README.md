# What if? preview

`preview.webp` is the What if? template's picture in New project: one frame of a real What if? film made in the app on
a DGX Spark. "What if the pull of the Earth switched off for ten seconds on a rainy neon-lit city road at night?" A
woman in a red coat floats over the street as water rises out of the puddles. She is a generated person, not a real
one.

It is frame 240 of `outputs/showcase-v2/whatif-gravity-vertical-final.mp4` (768x1280, the same-take final of the 9:16
film), scaled to 240x400 and saved as WebP at quality 75. Krea 2 Turbo drew the first keyframe, FLUX.2 Klein 4B the
other three, and LTX 2.5 made the film and its final. Licenses, as the recipe records them: Krea 2 Community License,
FLUX.2 Klein 4B Apache-2.0, LTX-2.x Community License. The `outputs/` folder lives in the main checkout and is not part
of the repo.
